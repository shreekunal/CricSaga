Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

CricSaga ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use the CricSaga mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your 10-digit mobile phone number, display name, playing role, and an encrypted password. We do not transmit or store passwords in plain text.
  • Match & Scoring Data: Cricket match data entered during scoring, including ball-by-ball events, player scores, extras, dismissals, and bowling figures.
  • Profile Media: User profile photos compressed client-side using WebP format to minimize storage footprint.
  • Device & Usage Data: Basic device technical specifications (operating system version, crash diagnostics) strictly to resolve bugs and ensure smooth scoring cockpit performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify player accounts and authenticate your login sessions.
  • To maintain grassroots match histories, team rosters, and career statistics.
  • To broadcast live match scores to teammates and followers.
  • To deliver tournament rankings, honors, and performance analytics.

3. Data Protection & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising networks. Passwords are protected using industry-standard hashing algorithms (bcrypt/Argon2id). Sensitive data transfers occur over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

4. Account and Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. If you wish to delete your account, remove your profile photo, or erase your match history, you can do so directly within the Settings menu of the CricSaga app or by contacting support.

5. Contact Us

For any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at support@cricsaga.app.
